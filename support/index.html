Support

Last updated 19 October 2026

Write to support@flytteklar.app — we usually answer within two working days. Include your phone model and app version (Settings) if something does not work.

Common questions

How do I change country or language?

Settings → Country and language. Country decides the tasks, deadlines and property types; language only translates. Changing country replaces the task catalogue but keeps your own notes, dates and completed tasks.

Why is a task missing or different from what my adviser said?

The catalogue is general guidance per country. Your contract, housing association or municipality can have other deadlines — add them as your own tasks or edit the date.

I had the app before Pro was introduced

Accounts created before version 2.0 keep every feature free. Open Settings → Flytteklar Pro to confirm.

How do I restore a purchase?

Settings → Flytteklar Pro → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

How do I delete my account?

Account → Delete account. This removes your online plan and account data permanently; local data on the device is deleted when you delete the app.

Sharing does not work

The invited person must sign in with exactly the e-mail address the invitation was sent to, within seven days. Ask the owner to send a new invitation if it has expired.
