Privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Who is responsible

Therkildsen Group, sole proprietorship, CVR 46203798, Teglkrogen 10, 8800 Viborg, Denmark, publishes Flytteklar (under the Ilumie brand) and is the data controller for the service. Contact: support@flytteklar.app.

What is stored, and why

Locally: your moving plan, addresses, tasks, budget, boxes, photos and attachments are stored on your device. Account: e-mail, chosen name and account ID are used for sign-in, online saving and shared plans. Online plan: if you turn on online saving, the whole plan incl. images is uploaded — signing in alone does not upload your local plan. Support: what you write to us is used to help you. Please do not put national ID numbers or other unnecessarily sensitive details in notes, photos or attachments.

Country and language

Your choice of country and language is stored on the device and decides which task catalogue you see. It is not shared with us.

Sharing

The owner manages invitations. A partner can see and edit the whole plan; a helper only gets the box overview and selected tasks. Invitations are bound to an e-mail address and expire after seven days. The owner can revoke access but cannot remove files others have already copied.

Services

Supabase handles accounts and online plans (database in the Stockholm region). Resend sends account-confirmation and password-reset e-mails (states that data is processed in the USA). Address suggestions send your search text to the national address service: Klimadatastyrelsen (DK), Kartverket (NO) or OpenStreetMap/Photon (SE). Purchases of Flytteklar Pro are processed by Apple or Google; to recognise your purchase across devices we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and receipts — never your plan. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. External links open other services with their own terms.

Permissions

Camera, photos and microphone are used only when you choose the feature and grant permission. Speech recognition may be processed by your phone's speech provider; Flytteklar stores the text, not the audio. Local reminders are processed on the device; web reminders use a push subscription at Supabase and your device's push provider.

No tracking

The app has no ads and no analytics. This website sets no analytics cookies; the hosting service (GitHub Pages) may log technical connection data.

Your rights

You can delete your account and all online data in the app (Account → Delete account). You have the right of access, rectification, erasure and portability, and may complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency. Account data is kept while the account exists; support e-mails for up to 12 months.
